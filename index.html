Privacy Policy for Kakuro

Last updated: 6 August 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Kakuro (com.richard.puzzle.studios.kakuro) on Android. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past — it is short because the app does very little with your data.

The short version

Kakuro has no accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own. Everything you do in the game — puzzles in progress, completion times, saved boards, and your settings — is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.

The one exception is advertising. If ads are shown, Google's advertising service receives certain information from your device, described below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, using Android's standard app storage. It is not transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to read it:

  • Your puzzle in progress, including entered digits and pencilled notes
  • Your completion times and their dates
  • Boards you have saved to the Replay library
  • Your preferences — theme, timer visibility, number pad position

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is nothing for us to delete on your behalf, because we never had it.

Advertising

Kakuro uses Google AdMob to display ads. When ads are shown, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares the following with Google:

InformationWhy
Advertising ID and similar device identifiersAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
IP address and general device informationAdvertising, analytics, fraud prevention
Interactions with ads (views, taps)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostic and performance informationReliability and fraud prevention

This information is handled by Google under its own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. Details of what the Mobile Ads SDK collects are published at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure.

Your gameplay — the puzzles you solve, your times, your saved boards — is never sent to Google or to any advertiser. Only the advertising information listed above leaves your device, and only while ads are being served.

Your choices about advertising

  • Android lets you reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time, and opt out of personalised advertising, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path varies by device and Android version).
  • You can also remove ads from Kakuro entirely by purchasing the one-time Remove Ads option in the app, if it is available in your region.

What Kakuro does not do

  • It does not ask for or store your name, email address, or any account details
  • It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or location
  • It does not track your location
  • It does not sell your information to anyone
  • It does not use analytics beyond what the advertising service described above collects

Purchases

Any in-app purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google records the purchase so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or change devices.

Children

Kakuro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please get in touch using the address below.
